About Vihara

VIHARA was born from a deep commitment to elevate academic and research excellence. It is a platform built to support scholars, empower institutions, and create meaningful academic impact.

Through webinars, seminars, conferences, research mentoring, and academic services, VIHARA strives to be a one-stop solution for academic growth.

VIHARA is not an individual effort — it is a collective vision driven by collaboration, innovation, and excellence.

Vihara Foundation

Vihara Foundation is the first initiative under Vihara Group, focused on academic development, research support, and training programs.

It marks the beginning of a larger journey toward building multiple impactful ventures under the Vihara ecosystem.

Our Vision

To build a dynamic ecosystem of initiatives under Vihara Group that collectively contribute to academic excellence, innovation, and societal impact.

We envision multiple ventures in research, education, publishing, and consulting — all aligned toward one goal: meaningful growth.
